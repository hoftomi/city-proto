# HouseCrest

The crest of a house of power: a shield with the house's tincture and initial.

**What the caller provides:** `tincture` (`voros` | `kek` | `zold` | `arany` | `bibor` | `fekete` | `narancs` | `szeder`), `initial`, `name` (for the screen reader), `npc`, `size` (default 28).

- An NPC house's crest has a dashed outline. In lists always complement it with the word „NPC”.
- The tincture is the house's color everywhere: on the influence bar, the map, routes.
